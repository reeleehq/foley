foley.index.taxonomy.resolver#

Resolve free tags/caption/labels to a UCS CatID (the EnvSound-UCS pipeline).

Staged precedence, highest wins (report 04 §5.3, [11 §3.1] — normalize heterogeneous labels onto UCS via mapping/synonym tables):

filename-parse > declared CatID > keyword/synonym > audioset-map > none

A declared CatID is one the file states about itself (the iXML CATID of a pro pack, foley.embedded_metadata); like a filename’s, it counts only when the table knows it. A keyword match weaker than an AudioSet-map hit (its band is below MatchPolicy.audioset_confidence) yields to one.

The keyword stage, in three pure steps over the table’s TermIndex (built once per table):

  1. collect — each term (subcategory, category name, synonym) found in one field of the input, word-boundary aware and across function words, becomes a Hit; the hits of one CatID fold into its Evidence;

  2. pick — the highest score wins (one word counts once, whatever it hits); on a tie, the stronger evidence, with a category name as strong as a subcategory, then the category’s generic (GENERAL/MISC) row, then table order;

  3. band — the confidence says how much evidence there is (MatchPolicy.band()), not which row won.

Everything tunable is one frozen MatchPolicy (policy=). The always-available default is this stdlib KeywordResolver; a richer CLAP zero-shot resolver (embed once, argmax over UCS label prompts, report 03 Part 2) can be dropped in behind the same TaxonomyResolver protocol.
