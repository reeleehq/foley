foley.repair#

Repair instead of quarantine: the fixes a Tier-0 QC report suggests, applied and re-checked (#107).

A paid generation (or any clip) that foley.qc.run_qc() would quarantine for a trivially repairable fault — a clipped run just over the gate, which usually rides with an edge click and a true peak over the ceiling — is worth a 20 ms fade and a true-peak attenuation more than it is worth the bin: the bytes were paid for. repair() applies exactly the fixes the report names, with foley’s own numpy primitives (foley.audio.fade(), foley.audio.limit_true_peak()), runs QC again on the result the way ingest will (foley.audio.to_working() then foley.qc.run_qc()), and says what it did. What it cannot fix it leaves alone, and says why (unrepairable()): NaN/Inf, silence, a too-short clip, or clipping heavy enough to be distortion — a flat top longer than about a millisecond, or more than a tenth of a percent of the clip — rather than a run just over the gate.

Only clipping quarantines a clip (an edge click and an over-ceiling peak are warnings, admitted), so by default the policy acts only on a clip QC would refuse; the fade and the attenuation then also clear the warnings it carries. To declick and attenuate clips QC admits with a warning too, lower the trigger: functools.partial(repair, min_status=QCStatus.pass_).

foley.generate() runs this by default (repair=True); repair=False keeps the quarantine, and a callable with repair()’s signature replaces the policy (an ffmpeg afade + alimiter repair, say). repair_bytes() is the same policy over encoded bytes — what generate calls, and the route for bytes a quarantine kept (foley.generations.get(key)). Every repair is recorded on the candidate’s notes and in the licence’s transformations, so the credit reads “(modified)” and the content credential carries an edit action.

Zero-dependency import: stdlib only at the top level; numpy (and soxr when the clip is not at the working rate, soundfile for repair_bytes()) load inside the functions.

Apply the fixes report suggests to samples and QC the result again.

Acts only on a clip whose status is below min_status — by default ingest’s admission floor, so only a clip that would be quarantined (clipping) — and whose faults a fade and an attenuation can fix (unrepairable()); otherwise the clip comes back untouched with reason set. Fixes, in order: a fade_s in/out declick when the report flags an edge click; a static true-peak attenuation to ceiling_dbtp when it flags a clip event or an over-ceiling peak. The re-check runs on the working array exactly as ingest does, so what clears here clears there.

Parameters:

  • samples (ndarray) – The clip to repair — the native-rate decoded array (mono or (frames, channels)), so what is stored afterwards is the repaired clip.

  • sample_rate (int) – Its rate in Hz.

  • report (Optional[QCReport]) – The clip’s QC report (default: computed here on the working array).

  • thresholds (QCThresholds) – The QC thresholds (default: the shipped table).

  • min_status (QCStatus) – Act on a clip whose status is below this (default: ingest’s admission floor, warn: only a clip that would be quarantined; QCStatus.pass_ also declicks/attenuates clips admitted with a warning).

  • fade_s (float) – Declick fade length in seconds.

  • ceiling_dbtp (float) – The true-peak ceiling the attenuation lands at.

  • max_clipped_ratio (float) – The most clipping still treated as repairable.

  • max_clipped_run (int) – The longest flat top (working-rate samples) still repairable.

Return type:

RepairResult

A RepairResult.

Lazy dependencies: numpy; soxr when sample_rate is not the working rate.

repair() over an encoded clip: decode, QC, apply policy, re-encode if it cleared.

What foley.generate() calls on a fresh generation, and the route for bytes a quarantine kept: raw = foley.generations.get(key); fixed, result = foley.repair.repair_bytes(raw). The QC pass here is the one ingest runs (the working array, the same thresholds), so a clip the gate admits as is costs one decode and nothing else.

Parameters:

  • audio_bytes (bytes) – Any clip soundfile decodes (the provider’s WAV/MP3/FLAC).

  • policy – True for repair() with its defaults, or a callable with its signature ((samples, sample_rate, *, report) -> RepairResult).

  • admit (QCStatus) – The admission floor a repair has to clear to be kept (ingest’s).

  • thresholds (QCThresholds) – The QC thresholds (default: the shipped table).

Returns:

the repaired clip re-encoded in the FLAC archive form when the policy repaired it and the re-check meets admit — a record made from it then describes this re-encode (format='flac', 24-bit), while the provider’s bytes as received stay where they were — else audio_bytes unchanged; and the RepairResult (result.note(admit) is the one-line account, None when there was nothing to do).

Return type:

Lazy dependencies: soundfile, numpy; soxr off the working rate.

Why report’s faults are beyond a Tier-0 repair — None when they are not.

Both clipping prongs QC fails on are bounded: the clipped ratio (a share of the clip) and the longest flat top (a length, so a long clip cannot hide a long plateau under a small ratio).
