foley.weave.broadcast#

A diegetic broadcast bed: speech lines heard through a radio/TV/PA/phone over a bed (#108).

A scene needs “the car radio”: a commentator’s lines over a stadium crowd, coloured as the device that plays them, looped for the scene. broadcast_bed() makes it in one call and returns a BroadcastBed whose licence carries the rights of both sources, narrowed, never widened:

bed = foley.broadcast_bed(
    [line1, line2],                      # speech already spoken (arrays, bytes, paths)
    bed_prompt="football stadium crowd", # or bed=<library id or audio>
    speech_license=speech_rights,        # the speech's provenance, as given
    duration=90.0,
)
bed.audio, bed.sr, bed.license

The pipeline is small pure stages, each its own function:

  1. speech — each line becomes a mono clip at sr (as_clip()); lines given as text go through the injected tts= seam first. join_lines() puts gap_s of silence after each line.

  2. bed — a library sound id, raw bed audio (with its bed_license), or bed_prompt through foley.generate() (budget, consent and licence rules unchanged; the generate= seam replaces it in tests).

  3. mix — mix_program() loops speech and bed to duration (the WEAVE fit_duration() loop with its equal-power seam), sets the bed

    bed_under_speech_db below the speech by integrated loudness, and ducks it (duck_envelope()): the bed holds that level while a line is spoken and swells up to -duck_db back up between lines (a gap shorter than a few release time constants does not reach it: ~9.2 of 10 dB with the defaults; duck_db=0: a constant bed).

  4. treatment — the whole program goes through the device (a radio colours the crowd it carries as much as the voice): a named TreatmentPreset from TREATMENTS (band-pass -> RMS compressor -> lookahead peak limiter), or any

    (samples, sr) -> samples callable.

Rights are checked first (mix_refusal(): no share-alike, no-derivatives, territory-limited or unmodifiable source on either side), then the bed is resolved or generated, and only then is any text spoken. A named generate backend’s licence row is checked before it is called; a bed from an unnamed generation order can only be gated on the clip it returns, so that refusal comes after the bed was paid for (never after the speech). The result always ends in the peak limiter, whatever the treatment.

The treatment is a seam. It is an audio “look” with no foley-specific knowledge, so its eventual home is the fleet’s audio package (mixing, beside mixing.loudness), not foley; until it lands there, foley’s in-house preset chain is the default (numpy + scipy, which foley[audio] already brings through mixing[loudness]; the limiter is mixing.loudness.limit_peaks()). Swapping in the fleet’s one is treatment=<its callable>.

Text-to-speech is a seam with no default. tts is any text -> audio callable (an array at sr, (samples, sr), encoded bytes, or a path); foley adds no TTS dependency. Two that exist in the fleet:

import voxy
tts = lambda text: voxy.text_to_speech(text, "commentator").audio  # encoded bytes

from an.audio.elevenlabs_tts import ElevenLabsTTS
eleven = ElevenLabsTTS(output_format="pcm_44100")  # a format soundfile decodes
tts = lambda text: eleven.synthesize(text, VOICE_ID).bytes_

The speech’s rights are never inferred from the TTS call: pass them as speech_license (e.g. a LicenseRecord for the TTS vendor’s terms). Without it the result is fail-closed: the speech counts as unknown rights.

Import cost: stdlib, foley.base and the numpy-free foley.weave.mix constants; numpy, scipy and mixing.loudness are imported inside the functions that need them, so import foley.weave stays dol-only.

foley.weave.broadcast.broadcast_bed(lines, *, bed=None, bed_prompt=None, treatment='am_radio', gap_s=0.6, duration=None, bed_under_speech_db=-12.0, duck_db=-10.0, duck_attack_s=0.02, duck_release_s=0.3, loop_speech=True, crossfade_s=0.05, ceiling_db=-1.0, speech_license=None, bed_license=None, tts=None, library=None, generate=None, generate_options=None, sr=None)[source]#

Speech lines heard through a radio/TV/PA/phone over a bed, looped for a scene (#108).

Everything that can be checked is checked before anything is paid for: the lines, the treatment, the bed’s form, and the rights of the speech and of a given bed (mix_refusal()). Then the bed is generated (if prompted) and gated, and only then is the text spoken.

Parameters:

  • lines (Sequence) – The spoken lines, in order: each an array at sr, (samples, sr), encoded bytes or a path; or text (a str that is not a file), which goes through tts=. Audio lines never go through tts.

  • bed – The bed: a library sound id, or its audio (any lines form).

  • bed_prompt (Optional[str]) – Instead of bed: a prompt for foley.generate() (its budget, paid-generation consent and licence gate apply unchanged).

  • treatment (Union[str, TreatmentPreset, Callable[[ndarray, int], ndarray], None]) – The device: a TREATMENTS name ('am_radio' default, 'tv', 'pa', 'phone'), a TreatmentPreset, any (samples, sr) -> samples callable (e.g. the fleet’s, once mixing has it), or None for dry. Applied to the whole program, crowd included.

  • gap_s (float) – Silence after each line (s).

  • duration (Optional[float]) – Length of the result (s); None: the lines once, with their gaps.

  • bed_under_speech_db (float) – The bed’s level under the speech (LU, integrated loudness), set before the treatment: the device’s compressor then narrows the gap a little, as a real one would.

  • duck_db (float) – How far the bed dips under each line relative to its level between lines (dB, negative): the bed sits bed_under_speech_db under the speech while a line is spoken and swells up to -duck_db back up in the gaps (short gaps fall short: ~9.2 dB with the defaults). Must be <= 0. 0: a constant bed (the behaviour before #253).

  • duck_attack_s (float) – Time constant (s) of the bed’s dip into each line.

  • duck_release_s (float) – Time constant (s) of the bed’s swell after each line.

  • loop_speech (bool) – Repeat the lines to fill duration (else they play once).

  • crossfade_s (float) – Equal-power crossfade at each loop seam (s).

  • ceiling_db (float) – The result’s peak ceiling (dBFS), held whatever the treatment (mixing.loudness.limit_peaks()).

  • speech_license (Optional[LicenseRecord]) – The speech’s rights, as given (e.g. the TTS vendor’s terms). None: unknown, so the result’s rights fail closed.

  • bed_license (Optional[LicenseRecord]) – The rights of raw bed audio given as bed (a library or generated bed carries its own).

  • tts (Optional[Callable[[str], Union[ndarray, tuple, bytes, str, PathLike]]]) – A text -> audio callable that speaks text lines (no default: see the module docstring for voxy and an’s ElevenLabs provider).

  • library – Where a bed id is looked up and a generated bed is stored (default: the process-wide library).

  • generate (Optional[Callable]) – The bed generator (default foley.generate(); a test seam).

  • generate_options (Optional[dict]) – Keyword arguments for it (backend, budget, duration_s, store, …; not library).

  • sr (Optional[int]) – Working sample rate (default foley.audio.WORKING_SAMPLE_RATE).

Return type:

BroadcastBed

Returns:

A BroadcastBed.

Raises:

  • BroadcastRefusal – A source’s rights do not allow it in (mix_refusal()).

  • ValueError – Unusable lines, treatment or bed form (before anything is spent).

  • KeyError – A bed id that is neither a file nor in the library.

foley.weave.broadcast.broadcast_license(bed, speech, *, treatment)[source]#

The broadcast’s rights: the bed’s, narrowed to the speech’s (never widened).

A copy of the bed’s licence, narrowed to the speech’s through the same lineage an edit records (foley.sources.edit.EditLineage: the permissions both hold, either’s attribution and notice, the stricter AI-use scope, and a transformations entry the credits and content credential read as “derived from” the speech). It is AI-generated, and its AI disclosure recommended, if either source’s is; the generator named is the bed’s, else the speech’s. Its bytes may be stored only if both allow it, and its rights are verified only if both are. The bed’s own watermark and content-credential reference are dropped: they described the bed’s bytes, not the mix. speech=None narrows to unknown rights. Raises BroadcastRefusal for a source mix_refusal() refuses.

Return type:

LicenseRecord

foley.weave.broadcast.mix_refusal(source)[source]#

Why a sound with rights source may not be treated and mixed in, or None.

The broadcast’s licence is the bed’s narrowed to the speech’s, so it cannot carry a share-alike obligation, and the device modifies both sources. Refused: a share-alike licence (on the record or in its lineage), a territory exclusion, a licence with no LICENSE_FLAGS row (an ND one among them), or one that forbids modification. Unknown rights ('unknown') are not refused: the result fails closed instead.

>>> from foley.licensing import apply_license_flags
>>> ok = apply_license_flags(LicenseRecord(source="s", license_id="CC-BY-4.0"))
>>> mix_refusal(ok) is None
True
>>> mix_refusal(LicenseRecord(source="s", license_id="CC-BY-SA-4.0"))[:40]
'its licence (CC-BY-SA-4.0) is share-alik'
